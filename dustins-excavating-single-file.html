Services

From the first cut to the final grade — six core services, one crew, no subcontracted digging.

Land Clearing

Before anything gets built, the lot has to be cleared. We remove trees, stumps, brush, and old fencing, then haul or chip the material off-site so it's not your problem to deal with later.

  • Selective or full-lot tree and brush removal
  • Stump grinding and root removal
  • Debris hauling and disposal
  • Erosion control measures during clearing

Site Preparation

A pad that's off-grade by even an inch causes problems for every trade that follows. We rough-grade, compact, and fine-grade the site so your builder starts on solid, level ground.

  • Rough and fine grading to plan elevations
  • Soil compaction testing and correction
  • Silt fencing and erosion control installation
  • Gravel base placement for building pads

Excavation & Grading

Foundation digs, basement excavation, utility trenching, and finish grading — cut to the elevations on your engineered plan and checked against it before we leave the site.

  • Foundation and basement excavation
  • Utility trenching for water, sewer, and electrical
  • Pond and retention basin excavation
  • Laser-guided finish grading

Demolition

Tear-downs for structures, slabs, pools, and pavement, with debris sorted and hauled so the site is genuinely clear — not just knocked down and left in a pile.

  • Residential and small commercial structure demo
  • Concrete slab and foundation removal
  • Pool and shed demolition
  • Debris sorting, hauling, and disposal

Drainage & Septic

Standing water and a failed septic system both come from the same root cause: water going where it shouldn't. We design and install systems that move it where it should.

  • French drains and catch basin installation
  • Culvert and driveway drainage crossings
  • New septic system design and installation
  • Septic repair and Title 5 inspection support

Driveway Installation

A driveway is only as good as what's underneath it. We build the base — grading, drainage, and compacted gravel or stone — to spec, whether you're finishing with pavement or leaving it as gravel.

  • Full driveway excavation and base construction
  • Gravel and crushed stone driveways
  • Grading and crown work for proper drainage
  • Culvert pipe installation at road crossings

Built on ground work, not shortcuts.

Dustin's Excavating started with one dump truck and a borrowed mini-excavator. Sixteen years later we run a full fleet — but the job hasn't changed: show up, do the dig right, and leave the site better than we found it.

Our story

Dustin Collier founded the company in 2009 after nine years operating heavy equipment for larger regional contractors. He started taking on small residential jobs on weekends — driveway culverts, drainage fixes, the work bigger outfits didn't want to schedule around.

Word got around. By 2013 the company had its first full-time crew. Today Dustin's Excavating runs three crews and a fleet of excavators, dozers, and dump trucks, but every estimate is still walked by someone from the family, and every job is still run by our own people — never subcontracted out.

We work across five counties in Central Massachusetts, split fairly evenly between homeowners, builders, and small commercial clients.

What guides the work

  • We tell you what we actually find

    Ledge, groundwater, old fill, buried debris — site conditions change the plan sometimes. We flag it immediately and talk through options before doing extra work, not after billing for it.

  • Our own crews, every time

    No subcontracted digging. The operator on your site is a Dustin's employee, trained on our equipment and held to our standard.

  • Written scope before the machines move

    Every job gets a written estimate with the scope, timeline, and price spelled out — so there are no surprises when the invoice arrives.

  • Clean site when we leave

    Debris hauled, equipment tracks graded out, and a final walkthrough against the original scope before we call a job done.
